Each case is one folder-stem with three text files: stage1/<stem>.txt, stage2/<stem>.txt, icd/<stem>.txt. The per-case review form (urgency, confidence, change driver, ICD pick, ICD confidence) is generated automatically — no question templates needed. Optionally include one allowed-list file (Name | Code per line, matched by filename anywhere in the upload): it names bare candidate codes and powers the searchable full-list picker shown when a rater answers “None of these”. When strict, cases missing any of the three roles are skipped.
